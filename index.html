Privacy Policy

App: Saudi Driving Test

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Saudi Driving Test handles information when you use our mobile application. We aim to protect your privacy and explain our data practices clearly.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Saudi Driving Test does not require you to create an account or sign in. You can use the app without registering.

If you choose to enter your name or select a profile photo, this information is stored locally on your device. Your practice progress and exam results are also stored locally to support app functionality. These details are not uploaded to our own server by the app's described local-storage features.

Information stored locally may be removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not provide cloud backup for this local data.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

Depending on the ad configuration and applicable settings, this information may include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • Ad views, taps, and interactions with the app.
  • Diagnostic information and app performance data.

This information may be processed by Google and relevant service providers under their own privacy policies and settings. We do not control all data processing performed by third-party advertising services.

For more information, please read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Information.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by the app or its advertising SDKs may be used to:

  • Save your practice progress and exam results on your device.
  • Display optional profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Display advertisements and support advertising measurement.
  • Help maintain app stability, security, and performance.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not operate an account system or our own cloud profile service for the local profile information described in this policy. Google AdMob may collect and share information as needed for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Profile details and progress stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to your device's settings and behavior.

We do not maintain a user account for this app, so there is no app account to delete. Advertising providers may retain information under their own policies and retention practices.

6. Data Security

We aim to limit information handling to what is needed for the app's functionality. Local information is stored on your device and its protection may depend on your device security settings. Data processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for people preparing for driving theory tests. It is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service used by the app, please contact us.

8. Your Choices

You can choose not to enter a name or select a profile photo. You can remove locally stored app data through your device settings. You can also review available advertising and privacy controls in your Android device settings and Google's services.

9. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob, which is governed by Google's own policies. Third-party services may update their practices over time. We encourage users to review the policies linked above.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
